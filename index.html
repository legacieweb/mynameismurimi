The company, in context

One company.
Four operating layers.

Iyonicorp is a digital business and technology company based in Nairobi, Kenya. It builds a family of products around business creation and the work that keeps a business running.

Rather than treating a digital business as only a website, Iyonicorp’s product family addresses the connected needs around it—from selling and payments to customer engagement, data, and productivity.

About the company

Built to make online work more connected.

Iyonicorp is a digital business and technology company based in Nairobi, Kenya.

The company’s role is to create useful businesses, build the technology around them, and keep improving how the pieces work together.

Iyonicorp serves builders, operators, and teams. Its work includes digital business concepts as well as products that support commerce, payments, customer relationships, automation, data, and everyday work.

The portfolio view is intentional: a business is more than a website, and its operating needs continue after launch. Iyonicorp’s products address different parts of that ongoing work, with the option to start small and add tools as needed.
